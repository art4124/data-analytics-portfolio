WHO I AM

People-centered by background, analytical by direction.

I'm Alanna Thornton, a psychology student building toward a career in data analysis. I'm especially interested in work that sits at the intersection of human behavior and data—using structured analysis to better understand people, improve systems, and support better decisions in healthcare, workforce, and behavioral settings.

My experience has consistently involved people, systems, and information: helping patients navigate behavioral health services, supporting members in financial services, coordinating student programs, collecting data, and contributing to analysis and program improvement. Those experiences have taught me to pay attention to both the details within a process and the people affected by it.

That background is what draws me to data analysis. I'm interested not only in what the numbers show, but in the human questions behind them: why patterns emerge, where barriers exist, what influences behavior and outcomes, and how evidence can be used to make systems work better for the people who rely on them.
